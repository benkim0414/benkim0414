# Naming the public engineering site

## Recommendation

Use **Engineering Practice** as the site/page title in place of
"portfolio", with the explanatory subtitle **Selected work, delivery
capabilities, and credentials.** For compact navigation, use **Work**.

This describes a public professional surface without reducing it to a gallery
of projects. It also leaves clear, descriptive section labels: *Selected work*,
*Experience*, *Capabilities*, *Credentials*, and *Contributions*. The subtitle
is important because W3C advises that headings and labels describe their topic
or purpose. [W3C WCAG 2.2: Headings and Labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels)

Suggested greeting copy:

> Welcome to my engineering practice. Browse the skills and evidence behind my
> work.

## Why this fits this site

The repository models public evidence across skills, learning, experience,
education, certifications, and projects, then connects it to DORA capability
dimensions ([`CONCEPTS.md`](../../CONCEPTS.md)). Its home page exposes DORA
capabilities with supporting experience, certifications, and technical skills
([`home-page.tsx`](../../apps/github.io/src/app/skills/home-page.tsx)).

That composition is closer to GitHub's concept of a **profile**—a place that
showcases work, contributions, and selected public information—than to a
projects-only collection. [GitHub: About your profile](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/concepts/personal-profile)
GitHub's job-search guidance likewise treats skills, professional experience,
projects, and achievements/certifications as distinct parts of a professional
presentation. [GitHub: Using your profile to enhance your resume](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/using-your-github-profile-to-enhance-your-resume)

The word *capabilities* is also precise for the DORA material: DORA's model
explicitly links technical capabilities to delivery performance and outcomes.
[DORA Core Model](https://dora.dev/resources/img/DORA_Postcard_2023-10.pdf)

## Alternatives

| Term | Best use | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Engineering Practice** | Recommended page/site title | Slightly less familiar than "portfolio"; retain the subtitle. |
| **Work & Credentials** | Conservative recruiter-facing title | Clear, but capability evidence and skills become implicit. |
| **Engineering Capability Profile** | A formal descriptor or About-page heading | Accurately signals DORA evidence; longer and more technical. |
| **Selected Work** | A curated projects/contributions section | Too narrow for the umbrella because it omits experience and credentials. |
| **Professional Profile** | A general-purpose personal site title | Familiar terminology, but it does not distinguish the evidence-led engineering focus. |

Avoid **Projects** as the umbrella: it narrows the site to one content type and
GitHub uses "Projects" for its planning/tracking product. [GitHub Projects documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects)
Avoid bare **Proof**: it sounds adversarial and does not clearly name the
content for visitors.
